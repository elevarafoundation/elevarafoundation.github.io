Corporate Partnerships

We partner with brands, agencies, and businesses to expand our impact. Corporate partners receive tax benefits, brand visibility, and measurable social impact. Here's how we work together:

💼

Program Sponsorship

Sponsor a specific program (portfolio grants, equipment library, workshops). Your brand funds 10–50 grants and receives recognition on our website and annual reports.

🎓

Workshop Hosting

Provide expertise through workshops or mentorship. Makeup brands teach product application, agencies share industry insights, photographers lead masterclasses.

📸

In-Kind Donations

Donate equipment, studio time, services, or products. Camera companies provide gear, studios offer free shoot time, brands contribute wardrobe.

🤝

Hiring Pipeline

Get first access to our talent network for casting, UGC campaigns, or creative hires. Foundation recipients are vetted, trained, and ready to work.
