Financial Transparency

100% of donations go to programs. Zero overhead. Complete public reporting. This is how we operate.

Revenue Sources

Individual donations, corporate sponsorships, grant foundation funding, and a percentage of future Elévara Store profits.

Program Allocation

Portfolio grants (40%), business grants (30%), equipment library (15%), space grants (10%), mentorship stipends (5%).

Zero Overhead

No salaries. No administrative costs. Legal fees and filing costs covered separately. Every donated dollar funds grants.

Annual Audits

Independent financial review conducted annually. Full reports published publicly on this website and filed with the IRS.

Board Governance

Quarterly board meetings. All grant decisions documented. Meeting minutes available upon request.

Recipient Tracking

6-month and 1-year follow-ups with all recipients. Track bookings, income, business sustainability, and career progression.

Annual Reports

Each year we publish a comprehensive impact report. These will be available here for public download.
