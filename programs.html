Portfolio Grants

$500 – $2,000 per grant

Professional portfolio photos are non-negotiable for models seeking agency representation or direct bookings. Yet shoots cost $1,000–$2,500 when you factor in photographer fees, hair and makeup, studio rental, wardrobe, and retouching. For emerging talent without savings or family support, this creates an impossible barrier.

Our Portfolio Grants cover the full cost of a professional shoot. We connect recipients with photographers from The Atelier network, coordinate hair and makeup artists, secure studio time, and handle all post-production. Recipients walk away with 15–25 publication-ready images suitable for agency submissions and comp cards.

This isn't charity — it's an investment. Grant recipients who complete portfolios secure representation or bookings at significantly higher rates than those with self-shot photos. We track first booking timelines and income earned in the year following the grant.

What's Included

  • Professional photographer (2-3 hour shoot)
  • Hair and makeup artist
  • Studio rental or location fees
  • Wardrobe consultation
  • 15-25 fully retouched images
  • Digital delivery + print files

Who Qualifies

Aspiring models (editorial, commercial, runway) who demonstrate commitment but lack financial resources for professional photos.

Application Process

Submit work samples (even if self-shot), goals, and financial context. Reviewed quarterly.

Business Startup Grants

$1,000 – $5,000 per grant

Talent needs infrastructure. Photographers need studios to launch portrait businesses. Casting directors need capital to register LLCs and build websites. Producers need equipment to open creative spaces. Stylists need startup funds to formalize agencies. The creative economy requires entrepreneurs — but traditional small business loans rarely serve creative ventures.

Our Business Startup Grants fund entrepreneurs launching talent-supporting businesses. Capital can cover business registration, website development, initial equipment purchases, first month's rent, marketing materials, or other essential startup costs. We prioritize businesses that will serve emerging talent at accessible rates.

Recipients submit business plans showing how they'll create opportunities for talent. We track businesses launched, services offered, and number of emerging creatives served in the first year. Successful recipients gain access to our network for client referrals and mentorship.

Eligible Uses

  • Business registration & legal setup
  • Website development
  • Initial equipment purchases
  • First month rent for studio/office
  • Marketing materials & branding
  • Insurance & licensing fees

Who Qualifies

Entrepreneurs launching photography studios, casting agencies, creative spaces, production companies, or other businesses serving emerging talent.

Required

Business plan, budget breakdown, and commitment to serving emerging talent at accessible rates.

First Booking Fund

Waived Platform Fees

Many talented models have portfolios but struggle to secure their first professional bookings. The Atelier platform connects talent with brands, but our 30% platform fee can feel prohibitive when you're still building momentum and haven't yet generated consistent income.

Recipients of the First Booking Fund get their first three bookings through The Atelier with waived platform fees. Instead of keeping 70% of booking value, they keep 100%. This gives them runway to build their reputation, secure testimonials, and establish cash flow before platform fees kick in.

After three fee-waived bookings, recipients transition to standard platform terms. We track booking completion rates, client satisfaction, and income earned. The goal is to launch sustainable careers, not just provide one-time support.

How It Works

  • Join The Atelier platform
  • First 3 bookings: 0% platform fee
  • You keep 100% of booking value
  • After 3 bookings: standard 30% fee
  • No repayment required

Who Qualifies

Models and creators with complete portfolios but no professional booking history or income.

Requirements

Must have portfolio (can be from our Portfolio Grant program) and complete Atelier profile.

Equipment Library

30–90 Day Loans

UGC creators need cameras, lighting, and audio equipment to build brand portfolios. Photographers launching studios need professional gear before they can secure paying clients. The equipment barrier stops talented creators from building businesses — you can't earn money creating content if you can't afford the tools to create it.

Our Equipment Library provides free 30–90 day loans of cameras, lighting kits, backdrops, tripods, audio gear, and other production equipment. Recipients use this time to build portfolios, secure their first clients, and generate income. Once they've earned enough, they can purchase the equipment outright or return it for the next recipient.

We track portfolio growth, client acquisition, and income earned during the loan period. Successful recipients often buy out their equipment at fair market value, which funds the library's expansion. Equipment returned in good condition goes to the next applicant in queue.

Available Equipment

  • DSLR and mirrorless cameras
  • LED lighting kits
  • Backdrops and stands
  • Audio recording equipment
  • Tripods and stabilizers
  • Editing software licenses

Loan Terms

30–90 day loans. Option to buy out at fair market value. Must return in good condition or replace if damaged.

Who Qualifies

UGC creators, photographers, and videographers committed to building professional portfolios and client bases.

Space Grants

3–6 Month Rent Subsidy

Creative businesses need physical space — photography studios, production offices, co-working spaces for talent, rehearsal studios. But commercial rent is prohibitively expensive during the launch phase when revenue is uncertain. Many talented entrepreneurs abandon viable business ideas because they can't afford the first 3–6 months of rent while building their client base.

Space Grants provide up to 50% rent subsidies for 3–6 months. This covers the critical early period when you're marketing services, securing first clients, and establishing cash flow. We prioritize spaces that will serve emerging talent at accessible rates — portfolio studios offering discounted shoots, creative co-working spaces with affordable memberships, production offices with equipment rental programs.

Recipients must demonstrate how their space will create opportunities for emerging talent. We track number of creatives served, services offered, and business sustainability after the subsidy period ends. Successful spaces often become long-term partners in our network.

Eligible Spaces

  • Photography studios
  • Production offices
  • Creative co-working spaces
  • Rehearsal studios
  • Casting offices
  • Multi-use creative spaces

Subsidy Terms

Up to 50% of monthly rent for 3–6 months. Must serve emerging talent at subsidized rates.

Requirements

Signed lease, business plan, commitment to accessible pricing for emerging talent.

Mentorship & Workshops

Free Access

Financial barriers are obvious — you either have money for a portfolio shoot or you don't. But knowledge barriers are just as significant. How do you price yourself? Navigate contracts? Build a professional network? Avoid exploitative clients? Manage taxes as a freelancer? These questions trip up even talented creatives.

All Foundation recipients get free access to our Mentorship & Workshops program. We host quarterly workshops on business development, contract negotiation, social media strategy, financial planning, and industry navigation. Topics are chosen based on recipient feedback and common challenges we see.

Beyond group workshops, we offer 1-on-1 mentorship pairing recipients with established professionals in our network. Photographers get guidance from working photographers. Models connect with experienced talent. Entrepreneurs get business coaching from creative business owners. Mentorship relationships last 6-12 months minimum.

Workshop Topics

  • Pricing & contract negotiation
  • Social media strategy
  • Portfolio development
  • Tax planning for freelancers
  • Client communication
  • Business development

Mentorship

1-on-1 pairing with established professionals. 6-12 month commitments. Monthly check-ins and ongoing support.

Who Qualifies

All Foundation grant recipients plus limited open cohorts for emerging talent not receiving other support.
