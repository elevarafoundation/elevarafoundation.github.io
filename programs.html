Portfolio Grants

$500 – $2,000 per grant

Professional portfolio shoots for emerging models and creative talent. We cover photographer fees, hair and makeup, studio rental, wardrobe, and professional retouching—everything needed to launch a career.

What's Covered

  • Professional photographer (2–4 hour shoot)
  • Hair and makeup artist
  • Studio rental or location fees
  • Wardrobe and styling (if needed)
  • Professional retouching (10–20 final images)

Eligibility

  • Ages 18–30 (emerging talent)
  • Demonstrable financial need
  • Serious about pursuing a creative career
  • Based in the United States

Business Startup Grants

$1,000 – $5,000 per grant

Capital for entrepreneurs launching photography studios, casting agencies, creative spaces, and talent-supporting businesses. We fund the infrastructure the creative economy needs.

What We Fund

  • Photography studio equipment and setup
  • Casting agency technology and operations
  • Creative coworking space buildouts
  • Talent management platform development
  • Production company startup costs

Requirements

  • Clear business plan focused on supporting emerging talent
  • Demonstrated industry experience
  • Commitment to accessible pricing for emerging talent

First Booking Fund

Free Access

Waived platform fees on recipients' first three professional bookings through The Atelier. Grant recipients keep 100% of their earnings until they're established.

How It Works

  • Portfolio grant recipients automatically enrolled
  • Zero platform fees on first 3 bookings
  • Recipients keep 100% of earnings
  • Standard fees apply after 3 bookings

Equipment Library

No-Cost Loans (30–90 days)

Free equipment loans for emerging photographers, videographers, and creative studios. Cameras, lighting, backdrops, and audio gear available for 30–90 day periods.

Available Equipment

  • Professional cameras (Canon, Sony, Nikon)
  • Lighting kits (strobes, continuous, modifiers)
  • Backdrops and studio accessories
  • Audio recording equipment
  • Lenses and specialty gear

Loan Terms

  • 30–90 day loan periods
  • Refundable security deposit required
  • Damage waiver insurance included
  • Pickup/return at foundation partner locations

Space Grants

Up to 50% Rent (3–6 months)

Rent subsidies for creative studios and production spaces serving emerging talent at accessible rates. We help studios offer below-market pricing to those who need it most.

What We Fund

  • Photography and video production studios
  • Creative coworking and office spaces
  • Rehearsal and performance venues
  • Equipment rental facilities

Requirements

  • Must offer reduced rates to emerging talent
  • Commitment to serving underrepresented communities
  • Track record of supporting creative professionals

Mentorship & Workshops

Open Access (Free)

Free workshops and 1-on-1 mentorship from established professionals in the creative industry. Business skills, portfolio development, client relations, and career navigation.

What We Offer

  • 1-on-1 mentorship with industry professionals
  • Monthly workshops on business and creative skills
  • Portfolio review sessions
  • Networking events and industry connections
  • Career planning and goal setting

Topics Covered

  • Building a professional portfolio
  • Pricing and negotiation strategies
  • Client communication and contracts
  • Marketing and social media
  • Business formation and taxes
